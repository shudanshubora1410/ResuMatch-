How the score is built

Six categories, each scored 0-100 by confidence-weighted keyword matching, then combined with per-job weights. Same resume and same job always give the same number.

  1. 1

    Extract and normalise

    PDF or DOCX text is cleaned and split into Summary, Skills, Experience, Education, Projects, Certifications and Other.

  2. 2

    Match skills with confidence

    A skill found in the Skills section counts more than one mentioned in passing. Synonyms such as "JS" and "JavaScript" resolve to one dictionary entry.

  3. 3

    Score six categories

    Skills, experience, education, projects, certifications and formatting each get a transparent 0-100 score with the matched and missing items listed.

  4. 4

    Explain and improve

    The breakdown lists plain-English reasons and what-if simulations show the projected score before you change anything.
